About

Indie Developer

waki1 is an indie developer building iPhone apps solo. Rather than working with a large team, I handle everything myself — from planning and design to development and release.

What I build are apps for keeping memories, managing your distance from information, tracking daily habits, and handling photos and video — small apps that aren't about flashy features, but about being quietly useful in everyday life.

I carefully review each feature, always thinking about whether it creates a comfortable experience for the people using it. Rather than flashy marketing, I care about the feel of actually using the app, and that's what drives my development.
